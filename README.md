# Spotify-Dashboard
# 🎵 Spotify Music Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

📊 Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze Spotify music data across songs, artists, albums, popularity, release years, duration, and monthly trends.

The dashboard uses Power Query, DAX, data modeling, and interactive visualizations to transform music data into meaningful insights.

# 🎯 Business Problems

The dashboard addresses key questions:

- How many songs and artists are in the dataset?
- Which artists have the most songs?
- Which songs are most popular?
- How are songs distributed across albums and singles?
- How does song volume change by year and month?
- What is the distribution of explicit and non-explicit content?
- How does popularity vary across album types?

# 🚀 Goal of the Dashboard

The main goal is to provide an interactive view of Spotify music data and help users:

- Monitor key music KPIs
- Identify popular artists and songs
- Analyze yearly and monthly trends
- Compare albums, singles, and compilations
- Understand content distribution
- Explore music data through interactive visuals

# 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Power BI – Dashboard development
- DAX – Measures and calculations
- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
- Data Modeling – Relationships and analytical structure
- Visualizations – KPI Cards, Donut Charts, Bar Charts, Line Charts and Interactive Navigation
- Data Source – Spotify music dataset

## 🎤 Artist Analysis

The dashboard highlights artists with the highest number of songs.

- Taylor Swift – 85 songs
- Travis Scott – 30 songs
- Drake – 27 songs
- Bad Bunny – 22 songs
- Beyoncé – 17 songs

## 🎵 Popular Songs

A dedicated visual highlights the most popular songs in the dataset, allowing users to quickly compare track popularity.

## 💿 Album Analysis

The dashboard compares songs across album types:

- Album – 562 songs
- Single – 269 songs

It also compares average popularity across album, single, and compilation categories.

## 📅 Time Trend Analysis

The dashboard analyzes songs by year and month.

- 2023 – 423 songs
- 2024 – 452 songs
- October – 220 songs
- July – 212 songs

## 🔞 Explicit Content Analysis

The dashboard provides a breakdown of explicit and non-explicit content:

- Non-Explicit – 17K
- Explicit – 11K

# 💡 Key Insights

- Taylor Swift has the highest number of songs among the artists displayed.
- 2024 has more songs than 2023 in the analyzed dataset.
- October records the highest number of distinct songs.
- Albums contain more songs than singles.
- The dashboard shows an average song duration of 3.28 minutes.
- Singles show the highest average popularity among the displayed album types.
